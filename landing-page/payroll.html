Zero Penalty

Statutory Compliance — ESI, PF, PT & TDS

TheBox auto-computes every statutory deduction. ESI is applied at 0.75% employee / 3.25% employer for gross salary ≤ ₹21,000. PF is calculated at 12% (employee) / 12% (employer) on basic up to ₹15,000 salary cap. Professional Tax slabs are pre-loaded for all Indian states — select your state and PT applies automatically.

  • All-State PT Slabs Built-in: Pre-loaded Professional Tax slabs for Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat, Tamil Nadu, West Bengal & 20+ more states — zero manual entry.
  • TDS & Loan Recovery Deductions: Toggle Income Tax (TDS), Loan Recovery, and other custom deductions per employee and let the payroll engine do the maths.
  • Fully Configurable: Set each statutory component as mandatory or optional, override percentages, and set salary applicability limits per your organisation's policies.
