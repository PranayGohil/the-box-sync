Quick-Tap Touch Interface

Clean and responsive design built for fast one-tap operations on tablets, POS terminals, and smartphones — zero lag during rush hours.

Dual Printing: Physical & Digital

Self-Service Dining

Contactless QR Ordering & Live Visual Menu

Eliminate order lines completely. Place dynamic QR standees on your counter or standing tables. Hungry diners scan with their smartphones, browse dishes with mouth-watering photos, select spicy/sweet preferences, and order directly to your prep station.

  • Instant Sold-Out Toggles: Run out of an ingredient? Mark the dish out of stock with one click across all QR menus instantly.
  • Direct Kitchen Punching: QR orders appear directly on your cashier screen and prep station with zero verbal miscommunication.
Kitchen Sync

Live Token Display & Station KOT Routing

Replace shouted orders and chaotic counters with synchronized digital order management. Orders punched from the POS or QR automatically generate running token numbers and route items to grill, fry, or beverage prep screens.

Sequential Token Numbers

Generate clear numeric tokens printed on receipts or displayed on an external pickup screen so customers know exactly when their order is ready.

Prep Station Segregation

Separate chaat, tawa, fryer, and beverage orders automatically to distinct kitchen tickets or screens for fast, organized preparation.

Stock Control

Ingredient Stock & Recipe Depletion

Food stall margins depend on controlling raw ingredient waste. Link raw supplies (buns, cheese, paneer, sauces, oil) to menu dishes. As items are billed, TheBox auto-deducts the exact recipe quantities from your stock inventory logs in real time.

  • Automated Component Deduction: Selling a wrap, burger, or pav bhaji automatically deducts butter, buns, and sauces from your inventory.
  • Low-Stock Notifications: Receive live on-screen notifications when critical raw materials drop below your safety buffer limit.
